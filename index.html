From discovery to decision

How to use this HipoBuy spreadsheet

Start with a product shortlist, then confirm the exact item and purchase conditions before paying.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose a specific variant

Search the directory and find the corresponding product on CAFANS. Record the color, size and model you want. Similar names can describe different products.

02 / VERIFY

Check the buying destination

Review the product detail page and its available platforms. If you choose HipoBuy, confirm that the destination shows the intended seller, variant and current price.

03 / REVIEW

Inspect before dispatch

Where warehouse inspection is offered, examine your own order photos and request missing measurements. Check the available return options before approving international shipment.

Budget beyond the product price

Plan the known costs

A spreadsheet price is one part of your budget. Enter your own quotes in USD to calculate a planning subtotal.

  • Use the price for your selected size, color and quantity.
  • Keep domestic delivery, agent charges and international shipping separate.
  • Check whether tax or import charges are included in a quote.
  • Treat an unknown charge as unresolved; a blank field does not mean free.

This tool adds entered amounts only. It does not retrieve exchange rates, shipping quotes or taxes.

Useful evidence to save

Make your shortlist easier to compare

Fit and measurements

For tops, compare chest width, body length and sleeves with a garment you own. For footwear, check the listing’s measurement method and sizing system. Save units alongside every measurement.

QC and product details

Save the exact listing and option beside inspection photos. Check visible condition, dimensions and included pieces. Photos of another order may help research, but do not confirm what your order will contain.

Availability and dates

Record when you last checked the price and stock. If a link changes or a seller replaces a listing, verify the new product before reusing your old notes. Keep unanswered questions visible.

Before you order

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to organize items for research and possible ordering through HipoBuy. This page provides an independent guide and a small CAFANS catalog selection, with local search, category filters and a CSV export.

Is this the official HipoBuy website?

No. This page is an independent discovery resource. Its HipoBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons open CAFANS, where you can browse products and review the buying platforms available for an item.

Are these real CAFANS products?

The 12 names and indicative USD prices were taken from the CAFANS homepage featured selection. This page is a static snapshot rather than a full catalog synchronization. Check CAFANS for the current product listing and available options.

Why can the final price differ?

The selected variant, seller price, currency conversion and additional charges can change the amount payable. Shipping and other fees are excluded from the reference prices shown here. Use the current destination quote when making a decision.

Does every product have verified QC photos?

No QC verification is claimed for this selection. Check whether inspection photos are available for the specific listing and your own order. Photographs alone do not establish authenticity, long-term durability or product safety.

Can I download this product list?

Yes. Use Download filtered CSV to export the currently visible entries, including their categories, indicative USD prices and CAFANS browsing destination. The export is a research snapshot, not a checkout quote.

Can I order or pay on this page?

No. This page provides discovery and planning tools. Open CAFANS, locate the product and review its buying options. Confirm the seller details, applicable charges and purchase conditions at the destination before paying.
